Account Deletion Instructions

Option 1: In-App Deletion

The fastest way to delete your data is directly within the Cadence app:

  • Open the Cadence app on your device.
  • Ensure you are signed in to your account.
  • Go to the 'You' (Profile) tab in the bottom navigation.
  • Scroll down to the bottom of the screen.
  • Tap on 'Delete my account'.
  • Confirm your choice in the popup dialog.

This process immediately removes all your data from our servers and your local device.

Option 2: Email Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion via email:

Subject: Account Deletion Request
To: nisar.uetm@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Cadence account. Requests are processed within 30 days.

What gets deleted?

Deleting your account is permanent. The following information will be erased:

Cycle & Symptom Logs
Health Reports
Medication Lists
Personal Preferences
Account Credentials
Cloud Backups
⚠️ This action cannot be undone. Once deleted, your health history cannot be recovered.
