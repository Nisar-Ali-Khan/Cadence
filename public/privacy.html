Privacy Policy for Cadence

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Welcome to Cadence, your PCOS and women's health companion. Your privacy is our top priority, especially when it comes to sensitive health data.

1. Data We Collect

To provide you with health insights and cycle predictions, we collect the following information:

  • Account Info: Name and Email (via Google Sign-In or Email registration).
  • Health Logs: Period dates, symptom intensity (pain, mood, bloating, acne, etc.), weight, and water intake.
  • Preferences: Theme choices and notification settings.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Calculate your cycle phases and predict upcoming periods.
  • Generate trend charts and health reports for your personal use.
  • Send you reminders (medications, hydration, etc.) as configured by you.

3. Data Storage & Security

We use Firebase (by Google) for secure data synchronization. This ensures your logs are backed up and accessible across your devices. We do not sell your personal health data to any third parties.

4. Biometric Security

If enabled, Cadence uses your device's local biometric authentication (Fingerprint or Face Unlock). This data stays strictly on your device and is never sent to our servers.

5. Your Control & Data Deletion

You are in full control of your data. You can:

  • Export your data at any time in CSV or JSON format.
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently through the Profile > Delete my account option in the app.
